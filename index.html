What is the Merchant Navy & Global Scope?

Commercial shipping is the economic engine transporting over 90% of global trade.

Commercial Global Fleet

Operate modern Container Ships, Oil & Chemical Tankers, LNG Carriers, and Bulk Carriers worldwide under international safety regulations.

High Tax-Free Earnings

Earn in foreign currencies (USD) with rapid salary growth as you climb through navigational and engineering ranks to Captain or Chief Engineer.

Rapid Rank Promotions

Structured DG Shipping competency examinations ensure merit-based rank elevations from Cadet to Management level.

Lucrative Shore Transitions

Experienced deck and engine officers smoothly transition to shore-based Port Agency, Vessel Operations, Technical Management, and Supply Chain hubs.

Maritime Courses & Ratings Guide

Detailed analysis of eligibility, scope, training duration, and verified ratings.

Degree Deck Path
★★★★★ 4.9/5 Rating

B.Sc. Nautical Science

3-Year Academic Degree (Deck Officer)
  • Eligibility: 10+2 with PCM (60%+) + English (50%+) + IMU-CET Rank.
  • Medical Standard: Strict 6/6 Eyesight without glasses; no color blindness.
  • Sea Training: 12 Months onboard Deck Cadetship post-college.
  • Shore Benefit: Full recognized Bachelor's degree eligible for MBA and shore corporate logistics.
Engineering Path
★★★★★ 4.8/5 Rating

B.Tech Marine Engineering

4-Year Technical Degree (Engine Officer)
  • Eligibility: 10+2 with PCM (60%+) + English (50%+) + IMU-CET Rank.
  • Medical Standard: Corrective glasses allowed (+/- 2.5D); normal color vision.
  • Sea Training: 6 Months Junior Engineer sea-time for Class IV COC.
  • Shore Benefit: High demand in power plants, industrial automation, and shipyard design.
Cadetship Route
★★★★☆ 4.6/5 Rating

DNS (Nautical Science)

1-Year Pre-Sea + 18 Months Sea Training
  • Critical Rule: Join ONLY with a confirmed Company Sponsorship Letter.
  • Leading Sponsors: Anglo-Eastern, Fleet, Synergy, TORM, Scorpio, BSM.
  • Advantage: Fastest pathway to Second Mate COC and earning in USD.
  • Unsponsored Risk: High waiting periods if unsponsored.
Crew / Rating Route
★★★★☆ 4.3/5 Rating

GP Rating

6-Month Pre-Sea Training (Deck / Engine Crew)
  • Eligibility: 10th Pass with 40%+ Aggregate & 40%+ in English.
  • Role: Seaman / Rating assisting in navigational watch, cargo, and engine maintenance.
  • Career Ladder: Progress from Ordinary Seaman (OS) to Able Seaman (AB), Bosun, and Officer via NCV exams.
  • Important Note: Only join DG Shipping approved institutes with placement records.
