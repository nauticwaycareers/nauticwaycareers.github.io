Courses & Pathways

Which maritime pathway are you considering?

These are common routes to explore. Always verify the latest institute and regulatory requirements before applying.

DECK

B.Sc. Nautical Science

Degree route focused on navigation, seamanship, cargo, safety and deck operations.

  • 3-year residential programme
  • Deck officer pathway
  • IMU-CET requirements may apply

DECK

DNS

Diploma in Nautical Science route designed around deck officer training and progression.

  • 1-year residential programme
  • Sponsored routes may apply at affiliated institutes
  • Sea training and certification progression

ENGINE

B.Tech Marine Engineering

Engineering pathway covering propulsion, machinery, maintenance and technical operations.

  • 4-year degree route
  • Marine engineering career
  • Medical and academic requirements apply

ENTRY

GP Rating

Rating-level maritime training for candidates exploring operational support roles at sea.

  • Deck/engine support roles
  • Training requirements vary
  • Check DGS-approved institute status

Eligibility

Check the basics before you apply.

Eligibility can change by programme, institute, category and admission year.

DECK PROGRAMMES

Typical academic checkpoints

  • 10+2/equivalent with the required science subjects for the chosen programme
  • For IMU B.Sc. Nautical Science 2026-27: PCM average minimum 60%
  • English requirement applies; IMU lists 50% in English for B.Sc. Nautical Science
  • Medical fitness and eyesight requirements must be satisfied
  • Age, marital status and entrance/sponsorship requirements depend on the route
BEFORE YOU PAY

Five checks every candidate should make

  • Confirm the institute/course is currently approved for the programme
  • Read the latest admission notice—not an old social-media post
  • Check medical fitness with an approved doctor where required
  • Understand total fees, refund terms and training structure
  • Verify sponsorship or placement claims independently
⚠️ Eligibility information on this page is a guidance reference. Requirements can change. Always verify the latest official IMU/DG Shipping notice before admission.
Career Progression

Deck and engine pathways.

A simplified overview. Actual progression depends on certificates, sea time, examinations, company requirements and applicable regulations.

🧭 Deck Department

01

Deck Cadet

Build practical sea experience and complete required training.

02

3rd Officer

Officer-level navigation, watchkeeping and safety responsibilities.

03

2nd Officer

Greater responsibility for navigation, passage planning and watchkeeping.

04

Chief Officer

Leads deck operations, cargo work, safety and deck-team management.

05

Master

Senior command role with overall responsibility for the vessel.

🔧 Engine Department

01

Trainee Marine Engineer

Build practical engine-room experience and complete required training.

02

4th Engineer

Officer responsible for assigned machinery and watchkeeping duties.

03

3rd Engineer

Progressive responsibility for machinery, maintenance and engine-room operations.

04

2nd Engineer

Senior engineering officer supporting engine-room management and maintenance.

05

Chief Engineer

Head of the engine department with overall machinery responsibility.

FAQ

Questions candidates commonly ask.

Is NauticWay Careers a shipping company or college?

No. NauticWay Careers is presented as a career guidance and mentorship platform, not a shipowner, ship-management company, college or recruitment agency.

Does NauticWay guarantee a job or sponsorship?

No. Admission, sponsorship and employment depend on the relevant institute/company, eligibility, selection process and applicable requirements.

Should I trust every Merchant Navy course advertisement?

No. Verify approval status, eligibility, fees, medical requirements, training structure and sponsorship/placement claims independently before paying.

Where can I verify official admission information?

Use official sources such as Indian Maritime University and the Directorate General of Shipping. Links are provided below.
